About Us

Two decades of building Sweden's best sports surfaces

Multi Sport Construction AB has specialised in sports field construction and maintenance since 2006, working on projects ranging from national football arenas to championship golf courses.

Our Story

Built on precision, trusted with the biggest stages

Founded in 2006 and based in Ekerö, Sweden, Multi Sport Construction AB has grown into a specialist contractor for sports field construction and maintenance. We work across five disciplines — football, golf, tennis, padel and athletics — bringing the same technical rigour to every project, whatever its scale.

Our football construction work includes pitches at Strawberry Arena, Stockholm Stadion and Olympia Stadion, built to the standards required for top-level competition. In golf, we've built some of Sweden's finest courses, including both the Castle Course and Stadium Course at Bro Hof Slott Golf Club, and Drottningholm Golf Course, among several others.

Beyond construction, we maintain both synthetic turf and natural grass football fields, helping clubs and municipalities protect their investment and keep surfaces performing season after season.

Our Process

From first site visit to final handover

Every project follows a proven process, adapted to the discipline and the site.

Site assessment & consultation

We review your site, requirements and budget to recommend the right construction approach.

Design & technical planning

Detailed plans covering base shape, drainage and surface specification for your discipline.

Groundwork & drainage

Base preparation and drainage networks built to support long-term surface performance.

Surface installation

Synthetic turf, natural grass, hard court or track surfacing installed to spec.

Line marking & finishing

Final markings, fittings and quality checks before handover.

Ongoing maintenance

Optional maintenance programmes to keep your surface safe and game-ready.
