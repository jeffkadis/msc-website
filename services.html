Our Services

Construction and maintenance for every playing surface

From football pitches to golf courses, tennis and padel courts, and running tracks — we handle every stage, from groundwork to final finish.

01 — Football

Football Fields

We build football pitches with either synthetic turf or natural grass, engineered to meet the demands of competitive play — including work delivered at Strawberry Arena, Stockholm Stadion and Olympia Stadion.

  • Synthetic turf pitch construction
  • Natural grass pitch construction
  • Drainage & sub-base engineering
  • Stadium & arena-grade pitches

02 — Golf

Golf Courses

We construct fairways, greens and full golf courses to championship standard, including both the Castle Course and Stadium Course at Bro Hof Slott Golf Club, and Drottningholm Golf Course.

  • Full course construction
  • Green & fairway shaping
  • Irrigation & drainage systems
  • Championship-standard turf

03 — Tennis

Tennis Courts

Precision-built tennis courts with reliable drainage and consistent surfacing, designed for club and private use alike.

  • Hard court & synthetic surfacing
  • Base preparation & drainage
  • Line marking & fencing
  • Resurfacing of existing courts

04 — Padel

Padel Courts

Fast-turnaround padel court construction, built to meet international dimension and surface standards for clubs and commercial venues.

  • Full enclosed court construction
  • Synthetic turf infill surfacing
  • Glass & mesh wall systems
  • Indoor & outdoor installations

05 — Athletics

Running Tracks

Durable, consistent running track construction for schools, clubs and athletics venues, built to support both training and competition.

  • Base shaping & drainage
  • Synthetic track surfacing
  • Lane marking
  • Track resurfacing & repair

06 — Maintenance

Turf Maintenance

We maintain both synthetic turf and natural grass football fields, helping clubs and venues protect their investment and keep surfaces performing at their best.

  • Synthetic turf brushing & infill top-up
  • Natural grass mowing & aeration
  • Seeding & feeding programmes
  • Scheduled seasonal inspections

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us about your site and we'll recommend the right construction or maintenance approach.
